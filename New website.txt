## IMPORTANT BRAND RULE

**Do NOT redesign the Shaka Tech brand from scratch.**

Keep the existing:
- Shaka Tech logo
- Existing colour scheme
- Existing font/typography
- Existing brand identity

The redesign should feel like a **premium evolution of the existing Shaka Tech website**, not a completely different company.

If the existing logo, colours or fonts are already implemented in the project, reuse them rather than replacing them.

Do not introduce random new colours that conflict with the existing Shaka Tech branding.

# MAIN OBJECTIVE

Transform Shaka Tech from a traditional web-development company website into a modern **AI-powered digital solutions company** focused initially on helping small businesses and tradespeople.

The main selling point should become:

> **We don't just build websites. We build websites that work for your business.**

Shaka Tech should demonstrate that an AI-powered website can:

- Answer customer questions 24/7
- Capture customer enquiries
- Collect customer information
- Qualify leads
- Arrange callbacks
- Book appointments
- Connect to the business owner's calendar
- Send confirmation messages
- Follow up with potential customers
- Help convert website visitors into customers

The website should therefore feel like a **demonstration of the technology**, not simply a portfolio website.
